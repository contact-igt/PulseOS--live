# PulseOS × Namokar — Quick start

**Sign in:** open the address your administrator gave you (it ends in `/login/namokar-v2` for the real pilot, or `/login/namokar-v1` for the practice demo). Use your email and password. Tick *Remember me* only on your own computer.

## The five things to know
1. **One enquiry = one Journey.** Click a name in **Leads** to open it. At the top you see who they are, where they came from (**Original source**), where they are now, who owns it and what happens next.
2. **Log every call** (**Log call** on the journey). A call where you spoke to the patient marks the enquiry *Contacted*.
3. **Always book from the journey** (**Book appointment**; only date and time are asked — the doctor and clinic are automatic). When the patient agrees, press **Confirm**: that sends the WhatsApp confirmation once and schedules the reminder 1 hour before.
4. **On the day:** *Check in patient → Move to waiting → Send to doctor*. The doctor completes the consultation and picks one result.
5. **No-show?** Mark it on the visit. PulseOS creates one follow-up for you in **My Work** — do not add another.

## Finding people
* **Leads** tabs: *Today, New Today, Uncontacted, Follow-up Due, Appointments Today, Appointment Booked, Missed Visit, No Response*.
* Typing a phone number in **Add Lead** tells you if the patient already exists — if so, it adds a new journey to the same patient (no duplicate).

## Where am I?
The line at the top (*Leads › Rajan Pillai › Cataract*) is your path. Click **Leads** to return to the same list you were on.

## Not part of this pilot
Revenue, payments and ROAS are switched off. The WhatsApp inbox is off. Messages and calls shown as *fixture* are demonstrations; nothing real is sent until the hospital's provider details are added.
